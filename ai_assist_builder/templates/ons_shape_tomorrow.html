Find out more about how we protect your data.

Before starting, please check that the letter is addressed to your household. If you do not live at the address on the letter, do not start the survey. You will need to enter the household access code from your letter to begin.

Why we might ask follow-up questions

To help us better understand your answers, we may sometimes ask extra questions based on what you've told us. Some of these follow-up questions are generated automatically using trusted technology. You can choose whether or not to answer them, and there's always a chance to give feedback if something doesn’t feel right.

2. Guidance on completing the study

Questions can be based on facts or opinions. No specialist knowledge is needed to take part. Your honest answers help us produce reliable statistics about society.

Your answers will be saved each time you move to the next page.

If you are not able to complete the study, the information you have shared with us is still useful. Saved data will be submitted.

For further information about our studies, our studies.

The Office for National Statistics run lots of different studies in order to produce official statistics. Find out more about studies about people, families and households.

3. Why you should take part

This study is carried out by the Office for National Statistics (ONS), the UK's largest independent producer of statistics. The ONS is not linked to any political party. Read more about what we do.

Taking part in this study is your chance to help us understand the changing needs of the whole community.

The information you provide is used to create statistics, such as official employment and unemployment rates. You can read about these in our statistical bulletins.

Government departments, public bodies and charities use our statistics for many projects, including:

  • improving health care, well-being, and social care services
  • removing barriers to work and supporting people unable to work
  • meeting the need for housing, school places and recycling services

4. How we protect your data

The information you provide will be combined with the data from across the country and will not be used to identify you or your household in any way.

All the information you submit is protected by law and treated as confidential under the Code of Practice for Statistics.

The ONS will receive all data collected and remain in control of all information given by participants.

For more information, see data protection and data protection and studies about people, families and households.

5. Use of automated follow-up questions

This study uses an artificial intelligence (AI) tool to generate automated follow-up questions. These questions are clearly labelled.

You can choose to answer, give feedback on, or skip automated follow-up questions.

You will only be asked an automated follow-up question if we need clarity on your previous answers. By providing an answer you will help us improve the accuracy of our statistics.

There is a small risk that automated follow-up questions will be inaccurate, offensive or ask for personal information. If that happens, please skip the question and give your feedback so we can improve the tool.

The AI tool uses an LLM (large language model) called Google Gemini that generates the automated follow-up questions. Your data is not stored by Google Gemini and it is not used to train Google’s AI models. It is only used once, for the specific purpose of creating the follow up questions.
